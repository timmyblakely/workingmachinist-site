Finishing operation
 
How this decision is made

Drill to size — general shop capability holds roughly ±0.003–0.005" with a Rough or Standard finish. If your tolerance and finish both fit that, a standard drill at (or near) final size is enough.

Drill + Ream — reaming typically holds ±0.0005–0.001" with a Ra 32-63 finish, and needs a pilot hole that leaves 0.01–0.03" of stock for the reamer to remove cleanly.

Drill + Bore — for tolerances tighter than about ±0.0005", boring is recommended, also needing a pilot hole leaving 0.01–0.03" of stock for the boring bar.

Mill to size — if no standard drill (inch or metric) lands within that 0.01–0.03" window below the finished size, there's no good pilot hole for a reamer or boring bar. Milling the hole to size (roughed with an end mill, finished by circular interpolation) sidesteps the problem entirely.

These are general shop guidelines, not a substitute for your own process capability data — adjust for material, hole depth-to-diameter ratio, and the tooling you actually have on hand.
